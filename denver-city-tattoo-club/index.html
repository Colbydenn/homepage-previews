A studio of one

Marco Vasquez has been etching graffiti-grade ink into Denver skin since 2014.

Born on the alleys of RiNo, raised in the wheat-paste flicker of Larimer. Every piece here is built the same way: a long conversation, a careful drawing, then a slow, deliberate session in the chair. Influences run from chicano fineline to wildstyle aerosol to the saints of South Broadway. Walk-ins welcome Friday through Sunday.

How it works

Four steps from idea to ink.

The conversation

Send a few sentences and any reference images. We'll talk through size, placement, style, and a rough budget before anything is drawn.

The drawing

A custom design is built for you alone. One round of revisions is included. Designs aren't shared until the day of the appointment.

The session

Plan for a relaxed, slow pace. Hourly minimums apply; large pieces are split across sessions to keep skin and stamina happy.

The aftercare

Detailed written aftercare goes home with you. A free touch-up is included within six months on most pieces.

Marco draws with the patience of someone who knows the piece will be on you for the rest of your life. Denver City Tattoo Club is the calmest tattoo studio I have ever sat in.
