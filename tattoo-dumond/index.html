A small studio

A quiet room in the Golden Triangle, run by Sylvie Dumond.

Tattoo Dumond is a one-artist studio. Every piece is drawn from scratch for the person who books it — no flash sheets, no off-the-shelf designs, no walk-ins. Sessions are slow on purpose. The studio holds one client a day, and the door stays closed when the chair is full.

How a piece is built

Four unhurried steps.

No flash sheets. No walk-ins. One client a day, every day, by appointment only.

i.

Inquiry

Send a few sentences and any reference images through the booking form. We'll talk through size, placement, style, and a rough budget before anything is drawn.

ii.

Drawing

A custom design is built for you alone. Drawings aren't shared in advance — you'll see them on the day of the appointment, when small adjustments still take minutes, not weeks.

iii.

Session

Plan for a long, slow day. The studio holds one client at a time. There's coffee, tea, a record player, and no rush. Most mid-size pieces finish in a single session.

iv.

Aftercare

Detailed written aftercare goes home with you. A free touch-up is included within six months on most work — ink takes time to settle, and we don't believe in last impressions made on healing skin.
