— A DENVER STUDIO · EST. 2018

Tattoos drawn from the natural world.

Think Tank is a Denver studio for nature-rooted, conceptual, illustrative work — botanical, mountain, river, fauna, and the quiet symbology that ties them together.

scroll
— APPROACH

Slow, considered, drawn for the body it goes on.

No flash sheets, no walk-ins, no copies. Every piece begins as a long conversation about the place, the memory, or the creature behind the request — and ends as a one-of-one piece worked across one or more deliberate sessions.

We work primarily in single-needle line, soft black-and-grey, and selective color accents — small to large, with a preference for compositions that move with the body, not against it.

Botanical

Native plants, ferns, mushrooms, fine line foliage with seasonal palette accents.

Mountain & River

Front Range topography, watercolor texture, geological cross-sections.

Fauna

Birds, mammals, fish — illustrative, soft-shaded, often woven into landscape.

— RECENT WORK

Sessions tend to run three to five hours.

Larger pieces are split across two or three appointments at a comfortable pace — rib pieces and back pieces especially. We send a redraw before every session and keep the door closed when the chair is full.

Color work is welcome. So is full-arm botanical scaffolding, scientific-illustration style. Cover-ups are case-by-case — bring photographs.

— BOOKING

Tell us about the piece you've been carrying.

A sentence about the subject, a hint about size and placement, and any reference photographs. We read every inquiry ourselves and reply within a week.
