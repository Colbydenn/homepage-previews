— THE STUDIO

Walk-ins on weekends.
Booked sessions on weekdays.

Three artists rotating, one chair each, no front desk. The buzzer is the doorbell. The cobblestone is wet most of the year. The neon stays on until the last needle is wiped.

We work small to medium pieces — cover-ups welcome, big back pieces by appointment only. No flash sheets glued to the wall, but we'll redraw your reference until it fits the body it's going on.
